# ParsianStore design quality

The visual language is a Persian workshop manual, not a generic marketplace
or SaaS dashboard. Every storefront change should reinforce vehicle fitment,
part-number speed, authenticity evidence, inventory truth, or expert support.

## Composition

- Build a page as a narrative, not a sequence of interchangeable cards.
- Use technical plates, ruled tables, inspection records, asymmetric editorial
  layouts, and deliberate negative space.
- Rounded bordered cards are reserved for genuinely independent controls or
  movable objects. Do not wrap every paragraph, row, or section in one.
- Alternate density and scale. A quiet evidence rail should follow a dramatic
  hero; a dense parts grid should follow an editorial explanation.
- Vehicle selection and OEM/SKU search are primary entry paths.

## Visual material

- Prefer real catalog media. A missing-image state must remain honest.
- Generated brand artwork may establish atmosphere, but must never impersonate
  a product photo, certificate, supplier record, customer, or business result.
- Exploded assemblies, technical grids, leader lines, inspection marks, paper
  grain, steel, rubber, and packaging are native materials for this product.
- Avoid sports-car glamour, neon cyberpunk, generic warehouse stock imagery,
  icon-in-circle feature rows, and decorative automotive clichés.

## Hierarchy and color

- Steel Blue behaves like technical ink: navigation, selection, verification.
- Marigold behaves like an inspection mark: commerce, price, decisive action.
- Graphite creates workshop atmosphere; light surfaces create catalog paper.
- Mono type is for real machine-readable evidence, not decorative filler.
- The Exploded View remains the signature interaction. Supporting imagery must
  stage it, not replace it.

## Review checklist

- Can a driver identify where to choose their vehicle immediately?
- Can a mechanic see where to enter a known code immediately?
- Does every trust claim show evidence or a concrete process?
- Is product media used when available, with no fabricated substitute?
- Does the page have at least three visibly different compositional beats?
- Would the layout still look intentional with all border radii removed?
- Are RTL, 360px, 1440px, light/dark, keyboard focus, reduced motion, and axe
  verified before handoff?
